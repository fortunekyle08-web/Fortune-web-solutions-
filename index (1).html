How the lead assistant works

Most customers contact the first business that replies. This makes sure that's you.

  1. A customer messages you

    From your website, a Facebook ad or WhatsApp, at any time.

  2. The assistant replies in seconds

    It asks what they need, how urgent it is, where they are and how to reach them.

  3. You get a ready-to-call lead

    A short summary lands on your phone, with urgent jobs flagged first.
